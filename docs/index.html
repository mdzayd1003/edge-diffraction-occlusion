Why the two curves should not coincide

Maekawa's chart comes from measurements, not from this theory, so agreement between them is evidence rather than circularity. UTD is known to sit slightly above it; across the default geometry the gap is 1.4–1.7 dB over seven octaves. A model that matched it exactly would be suspicious — it would mean one had been fitted to the other.

Two checks you can make with the sliders. Raise the receiver until the readout flips to line of sight: insertion loss collapses toward 0 dB, as it must once the barrier stops blocking anything. And at the crossing point the field is exactly half the free field — 6 dB — which falls out of the transition function rather than being fitted.

The JavaScript here is a port of diffraction/utd.py. It was checked against the Python across six geometries and all 24 bands; the largest disagreement is 0.0013 dB.
